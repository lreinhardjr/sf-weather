How this is counted

San Francisco counts as the coldest city on a day when it had the lowest overnight low of about 40 major US cities, and as the hottest when it had the highest afternoon high. It is about nights, not the city's famously cool afternoons.

Temperatures are modeled estimates for downtown San Francisco, not the official airport thermometer, and some wins were by less than a degree, so treat the count as a close estimate.
